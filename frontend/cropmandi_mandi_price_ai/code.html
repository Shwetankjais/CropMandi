⚠️ Important Disclaimer

All forecasts are generated from user-supplied AGMARKNET historical exports and have not been independently verified. They are experimental decision-support tools — not official market prices, not Coffee Board or Spices Board data, and not guaranteed sale quotes. Prediction uncertainty grows significantly for multi-day future forecasts. Always confirm prices with your local APMC/mandi before making a sale decision.
