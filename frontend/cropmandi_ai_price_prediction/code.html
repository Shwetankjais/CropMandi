Market history inputs

Important interpretation note

Tomato predicts a price-level target. The maize, paddy and potato models predict a delta, not a final price; add the delta to the appropriate baseline only after confirming the target definition. Tree-to-tree variation is diagnostic, not a calibrated confidence interval. The supplied artifacts do not record a forecast horizon or a verified unit.

Coffee and black pepper/cardamom are not listed here because the available trained-model bundle contains only these four pickle artifacts. Existing CropMandi price estimates for other commodities remain unchanged.
